# Nonlinear AC and full branch-outage hourly screening

S34. Full 16-year chronological nonlinear AC and fixed-dispatch N−1 screen
The previously selected 732-turbine portfolio and 2010–2025 140,160-hour fixed dispatch are passed to a synthetic 37-bus/93-branch positive-sequence balanced AC model. At every hour, five regional wind injections use reconstructed source wind time series and curtailment, and equal campus injections include PV/hydro, battery charge/discharge, HVO/B100 and dryer-booster load. Each campus retains idealized net reactive loading of 0.04 MVAr/MW (the 931 MVAr winter support assumption). A 1.0 pu synthetic external-bus slack is used; its resulting AC import/export is audited against the 2.5/3.0 GW limits. The complex nonlinear nodal PQ equations are solved directly by damped fixed-point current-injection iteration, and residuals are independently evaluated from the full complex Y-bus; nonconverged hours are counted as failures, not dropped. Branch send/receive-end apparent powers, bus magnitudes, resistive losses, and the external-grid slack are calculated from the solved complex voltages. Each of the 93 individual branches is removed in turn for every historical hour, in addition to N0. This is a fixed-injection contingency screen, NOT chronological AC optimal power flow or corrective N−1 redispatch: there are no post-outage changes to batteries, grid services, active generation, reactive controller setpoints or historical fuel inventories. Five full wind-region generation outages are also NOT part of the 93-branch outage result. Therefore a failure is evidence that the selected uncorrected operating point is insecure; a pass is only a property of this synthetic steady-state model, not grid approval. No historical BC Hydro topology, actual equipment impedance, protection, short-circuit or stability validation is implied.

## Results

13,175,040 nonlinear AC solves (140,160 hourly input states × 94 intact/single-branch states), with 0 nonconverged solutions. Of the 140,160 modeled hours, 48,766 fail an intact-network constraint and 101,640 fail at least one intact or N−1 constraint; 52,874 pass N0 but fail at least one outage. Across the 13,175,040 evaluated states, 4,585,414 exceed grid import/export limits, 193,812 exceed an assumed MVA branch rating and 0 breach the 0.95–1.05 pu voltage envelope (overlapping categories). The maximum modeled branch loading is 112.8% of the assumed rating, the minimum voltage is 0.951 pu and the maximum solved external import is 2508.53 MW. The mean intact-network AC resistive loss is 25.75 MW (about 225.6 GWh per 8,760-hour year). These AC quantities do not automatically replace the loss-aware I²R dispatch, because the battery/fuel chronology was not recomputed after each AC solve.

## Interpretation

The synthetic model has not demonstrated full N−1 feasibility under the existing dispatch. Corrective AC OPF, new transmission ratings, equipment Q limits, fuel inventory constrained redispatch, utility validation, and site-specific wind assessment are next steps. The numerical sweep does not certify any real BC Hydro system.
